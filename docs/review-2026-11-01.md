## Findings and implemented repairs

1. The original script offered an unearned cure and contradicted its distinction between
   reflection and repetitive thought. Added a saved editorial review and focused revisions.
   Editorial model judgments are evidence for review, not automatic factual certification.
2. Large script responses exhausted free output caps. Added outline/beat expansion in small
   requests and removed the writer's 1,500-token floor, which overrode smaller caller budgets.
3. Quotation marks alone are not invalid narration. Fictional dialogue and inner thoughts
   are allowed; unverifiable quotations attributed to real thinkers remain rejected.
4. The validator silently truncated narration at 150 characters. Narration is now preserved.
5. Captions lost contrast over light characters and exposed incomplete words. Added readable
   backing panels, phrase-sized captions and original-script words aligned to ASR anchors.
6. Cumulative “EDITS” counters reset between shots. Added explicit cumulative/value semantics
   and deliberate reset support; ordinary decreasing values are allowed.
7. Walk trajectories jumped at acceleration boundaries. Replaced discontinuous easing and
   used distance-driven planted stance feet, checked through the actual IK endpoints.
8. A café sitting frame had no supporting stool. Added real-seat alignment, scale correction
   and visible fallback support. Re-rendered snapshots showed the corrected contact.
9. A held cup remained duplicated on the café table. The attached cup now replaces its
   corresponding table instance.
10. Music loops concatenated without a crossfade; long smoothing convolutions were expensive.
    Added complementary crossfades and FFT smoothing.
11. Speech verification skipped twists and did not reliably verify regenerated takes.
    It now retains evidence for each spoken part, checks retries, audits disputed audio
    independently and protects negation. The agreement threshold was not lowered.
12. Thumbnails contained cropped subtitles, dialogue and counters. Added a separate clean
    illustration pass and corrected the accent-word color selection.
13. Added encoded-master codec/resolution/FPS/duration/loudness/decode checks, failure
    diagnostics, VTT export and research sidecars.

## Limits

- Audio review used ASR, timing and signal measurements. No claim of an actual listening
  assessment or guaranteed natural emotional delivery is made.
- The canvas production vocabulary is smaller than the older Python acting engine.
  Complex pickups, exchanges, collision handling and continuous acting across every cut
  are not universally solved by the new gait and seating repairs.
- Research currently provides Wikipedia background reading, not a peer-reviewed claim audit.
  Sources are real retrieved URLs, but their presence does not validate every generated assertion.
- A four-minute target produced a 3:33 baseline. A corrected six-minute film and a different
  philosophical structure have not yet been verified by this review.
- The small.en independent audit resolves ASR disagreement; it does not prove expressive
  narration or certify that every word is perfect.
- There is no guarantee of YouTube monetization eligibility.
