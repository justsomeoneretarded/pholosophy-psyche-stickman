In this animated essay we follow Alex as he confronts existential nihilism and discovers that meaning isn’t handed down from the universe—it’s built, one small project at a time. Through sketchbooks, coffee talks, and a shadowy personification of the Void, the film shows how personal creativity can turn emptiness into quiet fulfillment.

Watch the journey from confusion on an empty billboard to hopeful sunrise on a rooftop, and learn why self‑made purpose beats the search for cosmic significance. #Existentialism #Nihilism #SelfMadeMeaning #AnimatedEssay #Philosophy
