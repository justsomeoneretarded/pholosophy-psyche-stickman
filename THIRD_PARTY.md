# Provenance

Original drawings, procedural soundtrack and synthesized effects: GPL-3.0-or-later.
Piper engine: GPL-3.0-or-later, https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl
Default voice: separately downloaded en_US-ljspeech-high; model card identifies public-domain LJ Speech dataset: https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/blob/main/en/en_US/ljspeech/high/MODEL_CARD
Qwen3 4B: Apache-2.0 model, https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-4B
Ollama: MIT, https://github.com/ollama/ollama
faster-whisper: MIT, https://github.com/SYSTRAN/faster-whisper
Whisper model: MIT, https://github.com/openai/whisper
Pillow: HPND. Pydantic: MIT. NumPy: BSD-3-Clause.
FFmpeg with libx264 is typically GPL; inspect the installed build: https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html
DejaVu Sans uses DejaVu/Bitstream Vera font terms; installed by OS, not bundled.
Crossref records and abstracts retain applicable source terms; DOI/publisher links are included.

Lessac was downloaded during setup but is not committed or selected by default. Its model card points to a research dataset license, so it is unsuitable as an assumed unrestricted channel voice.
